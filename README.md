# HR Workforce & Attrition Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing employee data to understand workforce details, employee attrition, job satisfaction, monthly income, overtime, and employee retention.

Python was used for data cleaning, data analysis, and visualisation. Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard to present the findings and help understand employee attrition patterns.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyse the total number of employees and employees who left the company.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Study attrition based on age, gender, and marital status.
- Compare attrition between employees who work overtime and those who do not.
- Understand the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition.
- Analyse monthly income, work-life balance, and employee tenure.
- Create Python visualisations to understand the data.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Provide recommendations based on the analysis.

## 3. Dataset Information

| Details | Description |
|---|---|
| Dataset Name | HR Employee Dataset |
| Domain | Human Resources Analytics |
| Total Records | 1,470 employee records |
| Total Attributes | 35 columns in the original dataset |
| Target Variable | Attrition |
| Data Format | CSV |

The dataset contains employee-related information such as age, department, job role, monthly income, job satisfaction, overtime, work-life balance, and years of experience.

## 5. Project Workflow

### Step 1: Data Loading

The HR employee dataset was loaded into Python using Pandas. The dataset structure, column names, data types, and sample records were reviewed.

### Step 2: Data Cleaning

The dataset was checked for data quality issues.

The cleaning process included:

- Checking missing values.
- Checking duplicate records.
- Standardising categorical values.
- Handling invalid numerical values.
- Filling suitable missing values.
- Removing records where the target variable, Attrition, was missing.
- Saving the cleaned dataset separately from the original dataset.

### Step 3: Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was performed to understand employee characteristics and attrition patterns.

The analysis included:

- Dataset summary and descriptive statistics.
- Employee attrition distribution.
- Monthly income distribution.
- Attrition by overtime.
- Correlation analysis.
- Age and income comparison.
- Job role workforce analysis.
- Job satisfaction and attrition analysis.
- Multivariate analysis using a pairplot.
- Income distribution by attrition and overtime.

### Step 4: Python Visualisation

Nine visualisations were created using Python in Google Colab.

1. Employee Attrition Distribution
2. Distribution of Monthly Employee Income
3. Employee Attrition Rate by Overtime
4. Correlation Heatmap of HR Variables
5. Age vs Monthly Income by Attrition
6. Job Role Workforce Profile: Tenure vs Income vs Attrition
7. Attrition Rate by Job Satisfaction
8. Pairplot – Multivariate Relationship Among HR Variables
9. Monthly Income Distribution by Attrition and Overtime

These charts help to understand the distribution of employee data and relationships between selected variables.

### Step 5: Power BI Dashboard

The cleaned dataset was imported into Power BI to create an interactive HR analytics dashboard.

The dashboard covers employee attrition, departments, job roles, age groups, gender, marital status, overtime, job satisfaction, work-life balance, employee tenure, and monthly income.

DAX measures were used to calculate key performance indicators.

## 7. Key Findings

The following findings were observed in the project dashboards:

- The dataset contains 1,470 employee records.
- The dashboard shows 231 employees who left the company.
- The overall attrition rate displayed in the dashboard is approximately 15.7%.
- Sales Representatives show higher attrition among the displayed job roles.
- Employees who work overtime show a higher attrition rate than employees who do not.
- Attrition varies across departments, job roles, and employee groups.
- Job satisfaction and work-life balance are useful factors to study when analysing employee retention.
- Employees with shorter tenure may need additional attention from HR.

These findings describe patterns in the available data and do not establish that any single factor directly causes attrition.

## 8. Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following actions may help improve employee retention:

- Review workload and overtime practices.
- Focus on job roles with higher attrition.
- Provide mentoring and training for early-career employees.
- Improve career development opportunities.
- Collect regular employee feedback.
- Encourage better work-life balance.
- Review compensation and benefits for roles with higher attrition.
- Monitor attrition by department, job role, overtime, satisfaction, and tenure.

## 9. Conclusion

This project provided practical experience in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, Python visualisation, and Power BI dashboard development.

By analysing employee attrition and workforce-related factors, the project presents useful information for understanding employee retention patterns and supporting HR decision-making.
